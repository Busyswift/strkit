## [Unreleased]

### Added

## [0.1.0] - 2026-09-27

### Added
- `slugify`, `to_snake_case`, `to_kebab_case`, `to_camel_case`, `to_title_case`,
  `truncate` and `word_count`.
- `strkit` command-line interface with `slug`, `snake`, `kebab`, `camel`, `title`
  and `truncate` commands.
